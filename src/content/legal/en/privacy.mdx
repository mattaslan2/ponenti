## 3. What we collect

When you submit a form (the contact form, or "email me my result" on a calculator or the risk test), we receive:

- Identity and contact details: name, work email, company name, phone (optional).
- Your request: your message, the form language and your segment (you have a US company, or you sell to the US from Türkiye).
- Test and calculator inputs: your risk-test level and answers (yes/no and ranges only) and your payment-days range.
- Campaign data: UTM parameters from the link that brought you to the site.
- Consent record: a record that you ticked the consent box, with a timestamp.

While you use the site, the following is also created:

- Technical logs: IP address, browser and device details, the page requested, date and time (our host's server logs).
- Bot check: technical signals that Vercel BotID reads from your browser when a form is sent, and a hidden honeypot field in the form.
- Error reports: when something breaks, the error message, page address, browser and operating system (Sentry).
- Analytics, only if you accept analytics cookies: pages viewed, clicks, device and browser details, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, and the referring page (PostHog).
- Preferences: your cookie choice and your language choice.

If you open the calendar and book a call, we receive your name, email, the time you pick and any note you add through Cal.com. If you tap the WhatsApp link and message us, we receive your phone number and messages through WhatsApp.

The site never asks for tax ID numbers, EINs or financial documents. Please do not enter them in a form.

We do not collect the special categories of personal data listed in Article 6 of KVKK, such as health, religion, criminal convictions or biometric data. Please do not include them in your message.

## 4. How we collect it

We collect personal data electronically, by fully or partly automated means, through the website's forms, cookies and similar technologies, and server logs. Information you send by email, WhatsApp or Cal.com is also collected electronically.

## 7. Transfers outside Türkiye

Ponenti operates in the United States, and our forms send your information directly to us. Under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority's guide on transfers abroad, collecting data directly from you is not by itself a transfer abroad. Passing that data to our service providers outside Türkiye is a transfer. It is governed by Article 9 of KVKK, as amended by Law No. 7499 and in force since 1 June 2024.

- Adequacy: the Personal Data Protection Board has not yet issued an adequacy decision for any country. There is none for the US or the UK.
- Appropriate safeguards: we plan to sign the Board's standard contracts with our service providers and to notify the Authority within five business days of each signature (Art. 9(4)(c) and Art. 9(5)). [confirm with counsel: contracts not yet signed]
- Until those contracts are signed and notified: transfers rely on the explicit consent you give with the form's consent box (Art. 9(6)(a)) and on steps taken at your request before a contract (Art. 9(6)(b)). The law allows these exceptions only for occasional transfers. [confirm with counsel]
- Analytics: your data goes to PostHog only if you accept analytics cookies, on the same terms.

Possible risks: the Board has not issued an adequacy decision for any country we transfer data to, including the US. Their laws may not give you the same rights and remedies as KVKK, and they may have no supervisory authority equivalent to the Board. US public authorities may request access to data under US law.

You can withdraw your explicit consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect transfers made before it.

## 11. Your rights under KVKK

Under Article 11 of KVKK, you can apply to us to:

- learn whether we process your personal data;
- request information about that processing;
- learn the purpose of processing and whether the data is used for that purpose;
- know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom the data is transferred;
- have incomplete or inaccurate data corrected;
- have data deleted or destroyed under the conditions in Article 7;
- have third parties who received the data told about a correction, deletion or destruction;
- object to a result against you that comes only from automated analysis;
- claim compensation if you suffer damage from unlawful processing.
